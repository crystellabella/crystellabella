About CrystellaBella

We build the team.
We see it through.

CrystellaBella LLC is a versatile government prime contractor, minority-owned and headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida. We do not fit into a single category. We secure contracts across a wide scope of industries and deliver through qualified subcontractors and teaming partners. From agriculture and botanical manufacturing to furniture, technology, medical supplies, and wholesale distribution — we bring the contract, build the right team, and make sure it gets done. We are SAM.gov registered, active, and ready.
