Who we are

Capability through partnership

CrystellaBella LLC is a woman-owned, minority-owned government contracting company based in Jacksonville, Florida. We pair strong contract management with qualified subcontractors and teaming partners to deliver mission-ready solutions across facilities support, administrative services, travel, food, disaster response, and more.

Our model is straightforward: we hold the contract and manage performance. Our partners bring the execution, specialized expertise, and capabilities that help agencies meet their needs.

What we offer
  • Facilities SupportJanitorial, grounds, and building services
  • Administrative & OfficeOffice support and document preparation
  • Travel & HospitalityTravel management and hotel/motel services
  • Food & LogisticsFood service and emergency supply support
  • Disaster ResponseEmergency and disaster response services
  • Specialty PartnershipsQualified partners for broader requirements
Our capabilities

Mission-ready through partnership

We build the right team for each requirement, with a focus on reliable delivery and clear accountability.

Core capabilities

Facilities support, administrative services, travel coordination, food services, logistics, disaster response, and specialized teaming.

How we work

We manage scope, schedule, communication, and contract performance while our partners deliver the specialized execution.
